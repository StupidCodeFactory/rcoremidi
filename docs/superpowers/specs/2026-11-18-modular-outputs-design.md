# Modular outputs: one MIDI port per instrument, velocity per step

## Goal

Drive hardware synths that each sit on their own MIDI port of a MOTU MIDI Express XT: a Moog DFAM through an LPZW Tram 8 (Port 1, channel 10, gates 1–8 on notes C4–G4) and a second MIDI-to-CV unit, a Mother-32 and a Subharmonicon. One rcoremidi app plays all of them from shared clips and journeys.

## Changes

### Velocity per step

`Clip#note` and `Clip#chord` take `velocity:`, an array read step by step like the rhythm (it wraps when shorter). Without it, notes keep velocity 90. On the Tram 8, velocity sets the CV output paired with the gate, so a velocity lane is a CV lane.

```ruby
note 'C4', 16, [1, 0, 1, 1] * 4, velocity: [127, 0, 60, 90] * 4
```

### Output port per instrument

```ruby
RCoreMidi::Instrument.register :dfam_steps, 9, output: 'Port 1' do
  play 1..1000, :dfam_steps
end
```

- `RCoreMidi::Destination.named(name)` finds the destination with that name among all CoreMIDI devices. No match or several matches raise `ArgumentError` listing the candidates.
- The instrument resolves its output when it is created, so a wrong name fails when the app loads.
- Notes generated by the instrument carry that destination. Notes of instruments without `output:` go to the app's `midi_out`, as before.
- `Registrable.register` passes keyword arguments through to `new`.
- `Client` groups the notes due on a clock by destination and calls `send_packets` once per destination.

### Silence every port used

The C client remembers each destination it has sent to (up to 16, fixed array, no allocation). `silence`, Stop and `dispose` flush and send All Sound Off and All Notes Off to all of them, instead of only the last one.

### Demo app

`script/modular_demo` with the DFAM step-driver clip on the Tram 8: gate 1 into ADV/CLOCK, CV 1 into VELOCITY. The drum-voice clip (TRIGGER plus pitch CV from the second MIDI-to-CV unit) is added once that unit's port and channel are identified.

## Out of scope

- 32nd and 64th note resolutions: `DurationCalculator` treats every step as a sixteenth, whatever the clip's resolution.
- Legato and glide, CC lanes, sending MIDI clock to the hardware.

## Testing

- Specs: velocity lanes in `RythmSequence` and `Clip`; `Destination.named`; instrument notes tagged with their destination; `Client` sending one batch per destination; keyword arguments through `register`.
- C: a script that sends to two destinations, silences, and checks with the clock lab's `midi_monitor` that the first destination received All Notes Off.
- Live: the demo app stepping the DFAM from Logic's clock.
